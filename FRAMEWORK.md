# Reference decomposition - industry-grade, granular, grounded

Decode WHY a short-form video worked, sliced fine across EVERY craft layer, then extract the
TRANSFERABLE mechanic mapped to OUR channel. Grounded in `frames/` + `transcript_timed.txt` +
`color.json` + `meta.json`. NEVER generic; NEVER hallucinated. Label **OBSERVED / INFERRED / UNKNOWN**
on every line, with [timestamp] / [frame] / [color.json value].

## A. CONCEPT & PSYCHOLOGY
1. **Format/genre taxonomy**: talking-head | POV-demo | listicle | story/storytime | reaction | skit | b-roll-essay | transformation | tutorial | duet/stitch.
2. **Core promise vs payoff**: what the viewer is told they'll get vs what they actually get.
3. **Cognitive triggers** (name + locate [ts]): curiosity-gap/open-loop | loss-aversion | identity-callout | social-proof | authority | controversy | relatability | pattern-interrupt | Zeigarnik (unfinished).
4. **Target-viewer precision**: how narrowly is the viewer called out? (generic <-> "you who does X").

## B. HOOK (0-3s) - frame by frame
5. **Hook mechanism** (the specific lever, not "good hook") + exact on-screen text + VO[0-3s] + the first VISUAL.
6. **Scroll-stop driver**: what in the FIRST FRAME (the in-feed thumbnail) stops the thumb - bold text? a face/eyes? motion? color contrast? a surprising subject?

## C. CINEMATOGRAPHY & SUBJECT (slice fine here)
7. **Shot grammar**: shot sizes (ECU/CU/MS/WS), POV vs 3rd-person, camera movement (locked-off/handheld/push-in/whip), approx shot count.
8. **Subject / person composition**: subject placement (rule-of-thirds vs centered), headroom, eye-line & gaze (to-lens?), body language, wardrobe/styling, on-camera energy/charisma. (No person -> product/subject framing.)
9. **Color** (from color.json): palette (dominant hues, warm/cool), grade (filmic/flat/punchy/desaturated), saturation & contrast level, brand-color consistency. What MOOD does the color set?
10. **Lighting**: source (natural/window/ring/hard/soft), direction, mood (bright-cheerful / moody / clinical).
11. **Set / background / production value**: location, clutter vs clean, depth/bokeh, what the BG signals about the creator.

## D. EDITING, MOTION & SOUND
12. **Cut rhythm**: cuts/sec or avg shot length, transition types (hard/match/whip/morph), speed ramps, J/L cuts (audio leads/lags visual).
13. **Motion graphics & annotations**: text animation, arrows/zoom/highlight, emoji, progress bars, sound-reactive FX.
14. **Typography**: font, weight, size, color, stroke/shadow, position, caption style (karaoke word-sync vs block), how it carries the hook.
15. **Audio design**: music genre & energy, trending-sound usage, SFX (whoosh/ding), VO tone & pace, beat-sync of cuts/text. (Music *texture* from text+stills = UNKNOWN unless stated.)

## E. RETENTION & GROWTH
16. **Retention structure**: open loops & payoff timing [ts], re-hooks, payoff cadence, predicted drop-off points.
17. **Value-delivery pattern**: front-loaded | escalating | save-best-for-last.
18. **Engineered behavior**: Share | Save | Comment | Rewatch - which one + how (the algorithmic objective).
19. **Loop & ending**: seamless loop? CTA type (like/save/follow/comment-bait)?

## F. SYNTHESIS
20. **Title / cover / caption synergy.**
21. **WHY IT OUTPERFORMED** vs the niche norm - the differentiator (use outlier stats).
22. **TRANSFERABLE MECHANIC**: surface (this topic) vs replicable PRINCIPLE - ONE sentence.
23. **FOR OUR CHANNEL**: 1-3 concrete ideas (title + hook + shot list + caption + CTA), channel-FIT filtered.

## Discipline
- Ground EVERY claim. OBSERVED (in data) / INFERRED (hypothesis) / UNKNOWN (not captured: audio texture, true motion between frames, exact lens).
- Kill platitudes: "could this apply to ANY video?" -> if yes, cut or specify.
- Multi-ref (>=3): surface the COMMON mechanics = the niche's current winning formula.
