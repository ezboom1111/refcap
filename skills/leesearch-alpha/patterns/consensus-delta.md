# Consensus-delta pattern

## When to use
Loop step 0, before declaring your thesis. Alpha = your answer − the consensus answer. You can't measure the delta without first writing down the consensus.

## Procedure

1. **Find the consensus answer.** Search "[topic] best / top / recommended / 추천 / 순위" and read the top 3-5 results. The names/picks that appear in ≥2 of those results ARE the consensus.

2. **Write it down in one line.** Format: `consensus = "<the obvious/brand answer>"`. Put this in your thesis `--decay` field or as a `frontier_open` note. Examples:
   - `consensus = "삼성바이오로직스, 셀트리온"` (for Korean biosimilar leaders)
   - `consensus = "Notion AI, Cursor"` (for AI productivity tools)

3. **Measure your delta.** Your pick must be DIFFERENT from the consensus line. If your best candidate IS the consensus answer, you don't have alpha — you have a well-researched confirmation of what everyone already knows.

4. **Decay = when the delta closes.** Alpha decays when your hidden pick becomes the consensus answer (gets priced in / widely known). Estimate when.

## Anti-patterns
- Skipping step 0 and declaring a thesis without knowing what the obvious answer is → you can't tell if your conclusion is surprising.
- Picking the consensus answer and calling it alpha because you found "more evidence" → that's RECON, not alpha.
- Using obscurity as a proxy for alpha → an obscure company nobody cares about isn't hidden alpha, it's just obscure.
